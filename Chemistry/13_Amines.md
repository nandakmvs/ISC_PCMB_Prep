# Chapter 13: Amines

## Part 1: Nomenclature, Classification & Structure
### 1.1 Classification
*   Aliphatic and Aromatic Amines.
*   $1^\circ$ (Primary), $2^\circ$ (Secondary), $3^\circ$ (Tertiary).
### 1.2 Structure
*   Pyramidal shape, $sp^3$ hybridized nitrogen with a lone pair.
### 1.3 Preparation of Amines
*   **Reduction of Nitro Compounds:** $R-NO_2 \xrightarrow{Sn/HCl} R-NH_2$.
*   **Gabriel Phthalimide Synthesis:** Used strictly for primary ALIPHATIC amines.
*   **Hoffmann Bromamide Degradation:** Product has ONE carbon less than the parent amide.

## Part 2: Basic Character of Amines
### 2.1 Basicity (Crucial ISC Topic)
*   **Gas Phase Basicity:** $3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ > NH_3$.
*   **Aqueous Phase Basicity:** 
    *   Methyl amines: $2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ > NH_3$.
    *   Ethyl amines: $2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ > NH_3$.
*   **Aromatic Amines:** Weaker bases than ammonia due to resonance.

## Part 3: Chemical Reactions of Amines
### 3.1 Reactions (General)
*   **Carbylamine Test:** ONLY for primary amines (foul-smelling isocyanide).
*   **Hinsberg's Test:** Differentiates $1^\circ, 2^\circ, 3^\circ$ amines.
### 3.2 Electrophilic Substitution of Aniline
*   **Bromination:** Yields 2,4,6-tribromoaniline directly.
*   **Nitration:** Significant meta product unless protected.

## Part 4: Diazonium Salts & Assertion-Reasoning
### 4.1 Diazonium Salts ($Ar-N_2^+ X^-$)
*   **Sandmeyer & Gattermann Reactions:** Replacement by Cl/Br.
*   **Coupling Reactions:** Forms colored azo dyes with phenols/anilines.

### 4.2 Assertion-Reasoning Examples
*   **Q:** Aniline does not undergo Friedel-Crafts. **R:** Salt formation with $AlCl_3$ deactivates ring. (A and R true, R explains A).

## Part 5: Chapter 13 Summary & Conversions
### 5.1 Key Conversions
1.  **Nitrobenzene to Aniline:** $Sn/HCl$.
2.  **Methylamine to Ethylamine:** Step-up reaction via cyanide.
### 5.2 SJBHC/ISC Focus
*   Basicity order. Hinsberg and Carbylamine tests.
