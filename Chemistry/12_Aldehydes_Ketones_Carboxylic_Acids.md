# Chapter 12: Aldehydes, Ketones, and Carboxylic Acids

## Part 1: Core Concepts & Preparation Methods
### 1.1 Structure of Carbonyl Group
*   $sp^2$ hybridized, trigonal planar geometry, highly polarized.

### 1.2 Preparation of Aldehydes and Ketones
*   **Rosenmund Reduction:** Acyl chlorides to aldehydes using $H_2/Pd-BaSO_4$.
*   **Stephen Reaction:** Reduction of nitriles with $SnCl_2/HCl$ followed by hydrolysis.
*   **Etard Reaction:** Toluene to benzaldehyde using $CrO_2Cl_2$.

### 1.3 Preparation of Carboxylic Acids
*   Oxidation of alkyl benzenes ($KMnO_4/KOH$ heat) converts side chains to carboxyl.

## Part 2: Chemical Reactions of Aldehydes & Ketones
### 2.1 Nucleophilic Addition Reactions
*   Addition of Ammonia Derivatives (2,4-DNP test).

### 2.2 Reduction and Oxidation
*   **Clemmensen:** $Zn-Hg/HCl$.
*   **Wolff-Kishner:** $NH_2NH_2/KOH$ in ethylene glycol.
*   **Tollens' & Fehling's Tests:** Specific to aldehydes.

### 2.3 Reactions due to $\alpha$-Hydrogen
*   **Aldol Condensation:** For carbonyls with $\alpha$-H in dilute base.
*   **Cannizzaro Reaction:** For aldehydes with NO $\alpha$-H in conc. alkali.

## Part 3: Properties and Reactions of Carboxylic Acids
### 3.1 Acidity
*   Stronger acids than phenols due to equivalent resonance structures of carboxylate.
*   -I groups increase acidity, +I groups decrease acidity.

### 3.5 Substitution in the Hydrocarbon Part
*   **Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ) Reaction:** Yields $\alpha$-halo carboxylic acids.
*   Meta-directing in electrophilic substitution. No Friedel-Crafts.

## Part 4: Assertion-Reasoning & Competency Based Questions
### 4.1 Assertion-Reasoning
*   Aldehydes vs ketones reactivity. Acidity comparisons.

### 4.2 Distinguishing Tests
*   Tollens, Fehling, Iodoform tests. $NaHCO_3$ test for carboxylic acids.

## Part 5: Core Conversions & Expected ISC Questions
### 5.1 High-Yield Conversions
1.  **Toluene to Benzaldehyde:** Etard.
2.  **Benzene to Acetophenone:** Friedel-Crafts acylation.

### 5.3 Mechanism focus
*   Nucleophilic addition mechanism. Aldol condensation. Esterification.
