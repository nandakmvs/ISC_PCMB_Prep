# Chapter 10: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

## Part 1: Core Concepts, Classification & Nomenclature
### 1.1 Classification
*   **Based on Number of Halogen Atoms:** Mono, Di, Tri, Tetra halogen derivatives.
*   **Based on $sp^3$ C-X Bond:** Alkyl halides, Allylic halides, Benzylic halides.
*   **Based on $sp^2$ C-X Bond:** Vinylic halides, Aryl halides.

### 1.2 Nomenclature (IUPAC Rules strictly followed in ISC)
*   Ensure numbering gives the lowest possible locant to the halogen.

### 1.3 Nature of the C-X Bond
*   Halogens are more electronegative than carbon, making the C-X bond highly polarized.
*   Dipole moment order: $CH_3Cl > CH_3F > CH_3Br > CH_3I$.

### 1.4 Preparation of Haloalkanes
*   **Halogen Exchange:** 
    *   **Finkelstein Reaction:** $R-X + NaI \xrightarrow{dry acetone} R-I + NaX$.
    *   **Swarts Reaction:** Heating alkyl chloride with metallic fluorides ($AgF$) to form alkyl fluorides.

## Part 2: Mechanisms & Chemical Reactions of Haloalkanes
### 2.1 Nucleophilic Substitution Reactions ($S_N1$ and $S_N2$)
*   **$S_N2$:** One-step, inversion of configuration, $1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ$.
*   **$S_N1$:** Two-step, carbocation intermediate, racemization, $3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ$.

### 2.2 Elimination Reactions
*   Dehydrohalogenation (using alc. KOH) follows Zaitsev Rule.

### 2.3 Reactions with Metals
*   **Grignard Reagent ($RMgX$):** Formation requires dry ether.

## Part 3: Haloarenes - Preparation and Reactions
### 3.1 Preparation of Haloarenes
*   **Sandmeyer's Reaction:** Diazotization of primary aromatic amines followed by reaction with $Cu_2X_2/HX$.

### 3.2 Chemical Properties of Haloarenes
*   Low reactivity in nucleophilic substitution due to resonance and $sp^2$ carbon.

### 3.3 Electrophilic Substitution Reactions
*   Halogens are deactivating but ortho/para directing.

## Part 4: Polyhalogen Compounds & Stereochemistry Basics
### 4.1 Stereochemical Principles
*   Chirality, Enantiomers, Racemic Mixture, Retention vs Inversion.

### 4.2 Polyhalogen Compounds
*   Chloroform (oxidized to phosgene), Iodoform (antiseptic), Freons, DDT.

## Part 5: Competency Based & Assertion-Reasoning
### 5.1 Assertion-Reasoning Patterns
*   Heavy focus on $S_N1$ vs $S_N2$ structural reasoning.

### 5.2 Direct Conversions
1.  **Propene to 1-Propanol:** React with $HBr / Peroxide$, then $aq. KOH$.
2.  **Aniline to Chlorobenzene:** Sandmeyer reaction.
