# Chapter 14: Biomolecules

## Part 1: Carbohydrates
### 1.1 Classification
*   **Monosaccharides:** Cannot be hydrolyzed further.
*   **Reducing vs Non-Reducing:** Reducing sugars reduce Tollens'/Fehling's (all monosaccharides, maltose, lactose). Sucrose is non-reducing.

### 1.2 Glucose ($C_6H_{12}O_6$)
*   **Evidence of Structure:**
    *   HI yields n-hexane (straight chain of 6 C).
    *   $NH_2OH$ forms oxime, $HCN$ forms cyanohydrin.
    *   Bromine water yields gluconic acid.

### 1.3 Disaccharides & Polysaccharides
*   Linked by **Glycosidic linkage**.
*   **Sucrose:** $\alpha$-D-glucose + $\beta$-D-fructose. Hydrolysis yields invert sugar.
*   **Starch:** Polymer of $\alpha$-glucose.

## Part 2: Proteins
### 2.1 Amino Acids
*   **Zwitterion Form:** Dipolar ion in aqueous solution.
*   **Isoelectric Point:** pH at which amino acid bears no net charge.

### 2.2 Structure of Proteins
*   Linked by **Peptide bonds**.
*   **Primary Structure:** Sequence of amino acids.
*   **Secondary Structure:** $\alpha$-helix or $\beta$-pleated sheet.

### 2.3 Denaturation of Proteins
*   Secondary and tertiary structures are destroyed, but the **primary structure remains intact**.

## Part 3: Nucleic Acids & Vitamins
### 3.1 Vitamins
*   **Fat-soluble:** A, D, E, K.
*   **Water-soluble:** B complex, C.

### 3.2 Nucleic Acids (DNA and RNA)
*   **Nucleotide:** Pentose sugar + Nitrogenous base + Phosphate group.
*   **DNA vs RNA:** DNA is the chemical basis of heredity; RNA is responsible for protein synthesis.

## Part 4: Assertion-Reasoning (Biomolecules)
### 4.1 Assertion-Reasoning Patterns
*   **Q:** Sucrose is non-reducing. **R:** Reducing groups are involved in glycosidic bond formation. (A and R true, R explains A).
*   **Q:** Vitamin C cannot be stored. **R:** It is water-soluble. (A and R true, R explains A).

### 5.1 Common Definitions tested in ISC
*   Mutarotation, Invert Sugar, Zwitterion.

### 5.2 Differences frequently asked
*   DNA vs RNA. Fibrous vs Globular proteins. Nucleotide vs Nucleoside.
