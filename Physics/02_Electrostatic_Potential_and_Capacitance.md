## 3. Important Formulas & Conceptual Points
- Potential $V = \frac{W}{q}$
- Potential of a point charge: $V = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{q}{r}$
- Potential of a dipole: $V = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{p \cos\theta}{r^2}$
- Relation between E and V: $E = -\frac{dV}{dr}$
- Capacitance $C = \frac{Q}{V}$
- Parallel Plate Capacitor: $C = \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d}$
- With dielectric: $C = \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d - t + \frac{t}{K}}$
- Series Combination: $\frac{1}{C_s} = \frac{1}{C_1} + \frac{1}{C_2} + \dots$
- Parallel Combination: $C_p = C_1 + C_2 + \dots$
- Energy stored: $U = \frac{1}{2}CV^2 = \frac{1}{2}QV = \frac{Q^2}{2C}$

## 4. SJBHC Specific Numerical Problems
**Type 1: Energy stored and Common Potential**
**Q1.** A $600 \text{ pF}$ capacitor is charged by a $200 \text{ V}$ supply. It is then disconnected from the supply and is connected to another uncharged $600 \text{ pF}$ capacitor. How much electrostatic energy is lost in the process?
*Step-by-step Solution:*
1. Initial energy: $U_1 = \frac{1}{2} C_1 V_1^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times 600 \times 10^{-12} \times (200)^2 = 1.2 \times 10^{-5} \text{ J}$.
2. Charge on first: $Q = C_1 V_1 = 1.2 \times 10^{-7} \text{ C}$.
3. Common potential: $V = \frac{Q_1 + Q_2}{C_1 + C_2} = \frac{1.2 \times 10^{-7}}{1200 \times 10^{-12}} = 100 \text{ V}$.
4. Final energy: $U_2 = \frac{1}{2} (C_1 + C_2) V^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times 1200 \times 10^{-12} \times (100)^2 = 0.6 \times 10^{-5} \text{ J}$.
5. Loss: $U_1 - U_2 = 0.6 \times 10^{-5} \text{ J}$.

**Type 2: Dielectric Insertion**
**Q2.** A parallel plate capacitor with air between plates has a capacitance of $8 \text{ pF}$. What will be the capacitance if distance is halved and space is filled with dielectric of $K = 6$?
*Solution:*
1. Initial: $C_0 = \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d} = 8 \text{ pF}$.
2. Final: $C = \frac{K \epsilon_0 A}{d/2} = 2K \left( \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d} \right) = 2 \times 6 \times 8 = 96 \text{ pF}$.

## 5. Competency Based Education (CBE) - Assertion-Reason & Case-Based
**Directions:** For Assertion (A) and Reason (R), choose the correct option:
a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
c) A is true but R is false.
d) A is false but R is true.

**Q1.**
**Assertion (A):** The work done in moving a charge on an equipotential surface is zero.
**Reason (R):** The potential difference between any two points on an equipotential surface is zero.
*Answer:* (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation.

**Q2.**
**Assertion (A):** The capacitance of a capacitor depends on the charge given to it.
**Reason (R):** Capacitance is defined as the ratio of charge to potential ($C = Q/V$).
*Answer:* (d) A is false but R is true. Capacitance depends only on geometry and dielectric medium.

**Case-Based Question:**
*Read the passage and answer:*
When a dielectric slab is inserted into a charged capacitor disconnected from the battery, the potential difference across it decreases.
*Q.* What happens to the energy stored?
*Ans.* It decreases, as $U = Q^2/2C$ and $C$ increases while $Q$ remains constant.
