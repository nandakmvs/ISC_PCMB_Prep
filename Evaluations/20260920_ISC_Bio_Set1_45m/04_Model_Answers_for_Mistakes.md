# Model Answers for Missed Questions - Biology Test 01 (Sunday)

**Q2: Define Apomixis.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** Apomixis is a form of **asexual reproduction** that mimics sexual reproduction, where seeds are formed **without fertilization**. *(Note: "Asexual fertilization" is a contradictory term and must never be used).*

**Q8: Differentiate between Homologous and Analogous Organs.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** 
- **Homologous Organs:** Organs that have the same basic structural design and origin but perform different functions (due to divergent evolution). *Example:* Forelimbs of a whale and a bat.
- **Analogous Organs:** Organs that have different structural designs and origins but perform similar functions (due to convergent evolution). *Example:* Wings of a butterfly and a bird.

**Q10: Explain the Meselson-Stahl Experiment.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
1. Grew *E. coli* in a medium containing $^{15}NH_4Cl$ (heavy nitrogen) for many generations until the DNA became fully labeled.
2. Transferred the cells to a normal $^{14}NH_4Cl$ medium.
3. Extracted DNA after 20 mins (one generation) and centrifuged in a Cesium Chloride (CsCl) density gradient. The DNA formed a hybrid/intermediate density band.
4. Extracted DNA after 40 mins (two generations). It formed two equal bands: one hybrid and one light.
**Conclusion:** This proved the semi-conservative replication of DNA.

**Q11: State the Law of Independent Assortment.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** When two pairs of traits are combined in a hybrid, segregation of one pair of characters is **independent** of the other pair of characters during gamete formation.

**Q12: Differentiate between Active and Passive Immunity (with examples).**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
- **Active Immunity:** Antibodies are produced by the host's own body in response to an antigen. *Example:* Immunity developed after a natural infection (like chickenpox) or vaccination.
- **Passive Immunity:** Pre-formed, ready-made antibodies are directly given to the body. *Example:* Anti-tetanus serum (ATS) or antibodies in mother's milk (IgA).

**Q14: Explain the hormonal regulation of Spermatogenesis.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
GnRH stimulates the anterior pituitary to release LH and FSH. LH acts on Leydig cells to stimulate testosterone synthesis. **FSH acts on Sertoli cells, stimulating the secretion of some factors which help in the process of spermiogenesis.**
