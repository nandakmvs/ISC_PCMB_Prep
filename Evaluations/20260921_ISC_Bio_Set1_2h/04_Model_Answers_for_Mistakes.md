**Q12: State two fundamental differences between divergent and convergent evolution.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
1. **Origin vs Function:** Divergent evolution occurs when related species develop different structures to perform **different functions** / adapt to different habitats. Convergent evolution occurs when unrelated species develop similar structures to perform **similar functions**.
2. **Organ Type:** Divergent evolution results in **homologous** organs. Convergent evolution results in **analogous** organs.

2. It acts as a plant growth promoter.

**Q14: Differentiate between a test cross and a back cross.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
- **Back Cross:** Crossing the $F_1$ hybrid with **any one** of its parents (either dominant or recessive).
- **Test Cross:** A specific type of back cross where the $F_1$ hybrid is crossed **only** with the homozygous recessive parent (used to determine the genotype of the $F_1$ individual).

**Q17: Mention two structural adaptations seen in desert plants (xerophytes).**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** (Any two)
1. Leaves are modified into **spines** to minimize water loss via transpiration.
2. Presence of a **thick cuticle** on leaf surfaces.
3. Stomata are arranged in deep pits (**sunken stomata**) to minimize water loss.

**Q18: Explain the specific roles of GnRH, LH, and FSH in regulating spermatogenesis.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
- **GnRH (Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone):** Secreted by the hypothalamus, it stimulates the anterior pituitary to release LH and FSH.
- **LH (Luteinizing Hormone):** Acts on the Leydig cells to stimulate the synthesis and secretion of androgens (testosterone).
- **FSH (Follicle-Stimulating Hormone):** Acts on the Sertoli cells to stimulate the secretion of factors (like Androgen Binding Protein) that help in the process of spermiogenesis.

**Q22: A couple is unable to conceive due to oligospermia. Suggest an ART.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
*Note:* Oligospermia is a **low sperm count** (not the complete absence, which is Azoospermia). 
The best ART technique is **ICSI (Intra Cytoplasmic Sperm Injection)**, where a single motile sperm is directly injected into the ovum in the laboratory, bypassing the need for a high sperm count or natural motility to penetrate the egg.

**Q23: Briefly describe the Miller-Urey experiment. What did it prove?**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
Miller and Urey created a closed flask containing **$CH_4$, $H_2$, $NH_3$, and water vapour** at $800^\circ C$. They simulated lightning by passing an electric **spark discharge** through tungsten electrodes. After a week, they observed the formation of simple amino acids. 
*Conclusion:* It proved the theory of chemical evolution (abiogenesis)—that simple organic monomers could form spontaneously from inorganic molecules under primitive Earth conditions.
