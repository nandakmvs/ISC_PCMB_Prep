# Model Answers for Missed Questions - Biology Test 03 (Morning)

**Q4: Name the specific immune response responsible for graft rejection.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** Cell-mediated immune response (CMI) mediated by **T-lymphocytes**.

**Q9: Name the infective stage of Plasmodium that enters the human body through a mosquito bite.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** **Sporozoites**.

**Q12: What is the term for the failure of homologous chromosomes to segregate during cell division?**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** **Non-disjunction**. *(Note: Aneuploidy is the resulting condition, but non-disjunction is the actual process of failure to segregate).*

**Q17: Differentiate between a Test Cross and an Out Cross.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
- **Test Cross:** Cross between an $F_1$ hybrid and its **homozygous recessive** parent.
- **Out Cross:** Cross between an $F_1$ hybrid and its **homozygous dominant** parent. *(Note: "Back cross" is an umbrella term for crossing with ANY parent).*

**Q29: Explain the process of secondary treatment (biological treatment) of sewage water, highlighting the role of 'flocs'.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
The primary effluent is passed into large aeration tanks where it is **mechanically agitated** (not centrifuged!) and air is pumped into it. This allows vigorous growth of useful aerobic microbes into **flocs** (masses of bacteria associated with fungal filaments to form mesh-like structures). While growing, these microbes consume the major part of the organic matter in the effluent, significantly reducing the BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand).

**Q30: Discuss three different types of innate immunity barriers in the human body with one example of each.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** *(You must provide exactly three if asked for three!)*
1. **Physical barriers:** Prevent entry of microorganisms. Example: Skin or mucus coating of respiratory tract.
2. **Physiological barriers:** Prevent microbial growth. Example: Acid (HCl) in the stomach or tears from eyes.
3. **Cellular barriers:** Phagocytose and destroy microbes. Example: Polymorphonuclear leukocytes (PMNL-neutrophils) or Macrophages.
*(Alternative 4th option: **Cytokine barriers** - Virus-infected cells secrete proteins called interferons).*

**Q31: What is linkage? How did Morgan explain the deviation from Mendel's 9:3:3:1 ratio in his dihybrid crosses?**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
- **Linkage:** The physical association or connection of two or more genes located on the same chromosome, causing them to be inherited together.
- **Morgan's Explanation:** Morgan attributed the deviation from the 9:3:3:1 ratio to the physical association of the two genes. Because the genes were linked on the same chromosome, the proportion of **parental gene combinations was much higher** than the non-parental (recombinant) types.
