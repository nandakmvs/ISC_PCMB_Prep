## SECTION A: Objective Questions (10 Marks)
*Answer all questions. Each question carries 1 mark.*

**1.** A charge $Q$ is divided into two parts $q$ and $Q-q$ and separated by a distance $R$. The force of repulsion between them will be maximum when:
(a) $q = Q/4$
(b) $q = Q/2$
(c) $q = Q$
(d) Cannot be determined

**2.** The electric potential at the surface of a charged spherical shell of radius $10 \text{ cm}$ is $50 \text{ V}$. What is the potential at a distance of $5 \text{ cm}$ from the center?
(a) $0 \text{ V}$
(b) $25 \text{ V}$
(c) $50 \text{ V}$
(d) $100 \text{ V}$

**3.** A wire of resistance $R$ is stretched to twice its original length. Its new resistance will be:
(a) $R/2$
(b) $2R$
(c) $4R$
(d) $R/4$

**4.** When a glass prism is immersed in water, its dispersive power:
(a) Increases
(b) Decreases
(c) Remains unchanged
(d) Becomes zero

**5.** In Young's Double Slit Experiment, if one of the slits is covered by a transparent glass plate of refractive index $1.5$, the central fringe will:
(a) Remain at the same position
(b) Shift towards the covered slit
(c) Shift away from the covered slit
(d) Disappear

**6.** Which of the following wavefronts is associated with a line source at a finite distance?
(a) Spherical
(b) Cylindrical
(c) Plane
(d) Elliptical

**7.** A point charge $+q$ is placed at the center of a cube of side $L$. The electric flux emerging from any one face of the cube is:
(a) $\frac{q}{\epsilon_0}$
(b) $\frac{q}{6\epsilon_0}$
(c) $\frac{q}{4\epsilon_0}$
(d) Zero

**8.** Three capacitors each of capacity $C$ are connected in such a way that the effective capacitance is $2C/3$. This is done by connecting:
(a) All three in series
(b) All three in parallel
(c) Two in series and one in parallel
(d) Two in parallel and one in series

**9.** The temperature coefficient of resistance of a semiconductor is:
(a) Positive
(b) Negative
(c) Zero
(d) Infinite

**10.** According to Gauss's Law, if the net electric flux through a closed surface is zero, then:
(a) No charges exist anywhere in space
(b) The net charge enclosed by the surface is zero
(c) The electric field must be zero everywhere on the surface
(d) The surface must be a sphere

## SECTION C: Short Answer Type II (15 Marks)
*Answer all questions. Each question carries 3 marks.*

**16.** Using Gauss's Law, derive an expression for the electric field intensity due to a uniformly charged thin spherical shell at a point outside the shell. **(Must include a labeled diagram).**
