**Perfect ISC Answer:** Contact inhibition. *(Note: "Growth inhibition" is too generic).*

**Q26: Briefly describe the biological principle behind the use of Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) in agriculture.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:** Bt produces protein crystals during a particular phase of its growth which contain a toxic insecticidal protein. It exists as an **inactive protoxin**, but once an insect ingests it, it is converted into an **active toxin due to the alkaline pH of the insect gut**. The active toxin binds to the surface of midgut epithelial cells and creates pores that cause cell swelling and lysis, eventually killing the insect.

**Q27: Differentiate between Primary and Secondary lymphoid organs with one example of each.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
- **Primary Lymphoid Organs:** Where immature lymphocytes differentiate into antigen-sensitive lymphocytes. Example: **Bone marrow** or **Thymus**.
- **Secondary Lymphoid Organs:** Where lymphocytes interact with the antigen and proliferate to become effector cells. Example: **Spleen** or **Lymph nodes**.

**Q31: Describe the three main types of endosperm development in angiosperms.**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
1. **Nuclear Endosperm:** The primary endosperm nucleus undergoes successive nuclear divisions without cell wall formation, resulting in a free-nuclear stage (e.g., coconut water).
2. **Cellular Endosperm:** Cell wall formation occurs immediately after every nuclear division, making the endosperm entirely cellular from the start (e.g., white kernel of coconut, Petunia).
3. **Helobial Endosperm:** An intermediate type where the first division is followed by transverse wall formation, creating two unequal chambers, and subsequent divisions are free-nuclear (e.g., Order Helobiales).

**Q32: What is Artificial Insemination (AI)? Differentiate between AI and Intra Uterine Insemination (IUI). Under what conditions are these techniques recommended?**
**Perfect ISC Answer:**
- **AI:** Semen collected from the husband or a healthy donor is artificially introduced into the vagina of the female.
- **IUI:** The collected semen is artificially introduced directly into the uterus of the female.
- **Conditions recommended:** These techniques are used when the male partner is **unable to inseminate the female** (e.g., erectile dysfunction) or if he has a **very low sperm count** (oligospermia) in his ejaculates.
