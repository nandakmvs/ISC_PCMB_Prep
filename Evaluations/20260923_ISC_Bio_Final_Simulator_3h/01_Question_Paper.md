## SECTION A (20 Marks)
*Answer all questions. Each question carries 1 mark.*

**Question 1:** Name the specific step in the HIV replication cycle where the newly formed virus particles exit the host macrophage, turning it into a viral factory.

**Question 3:** Name the type of pollination where pollen grains from one flower are transferred to the stigma of another flower on the *same* plant.
**Question 4:** Name the hormone responsible for maintaining the endometrium during pregnancy, primarily secreted by the corpus luteum.

**Question 6:** Name the specific type of immunity provided by the transfer of colostrum (containing IgA) from mother to infant.

**Question 9:** Which genetic disorder is characterized by the presence of a single X chromosome (44A + XO) in females?
**Question 10:** Name the physical connection or association of two or more genes located on the same chromosome.
**Question 11:** What is the term for the amount of oxygen required by bacteria to oxidize all organic matter in one liter of water?
**Question 12:** Name the specific region of the angiosperm ovule where the funicle is attached to the body of the ovule.
**Question 13:** Give one example of a genetic trait in humans that exhibits Polygenic Inheritance.
**Question 14:** Name the temporary endocrine gland formed in the ovary after ovulation.
**Question 15:** Which specific property of normal cells prevents them from growing uncontrollably when they come into contact with other cells?
**Question 16:** Name the ecological interaction where one species is harmed and the other is unaffected.
**Question 17:** What is the specific role of the enzyme *Integrase* during an HIV infection?
**Question 18:** Which phase of population growth is represented by the asymptote in a logistic growth curve?
**Question 19:** Name the non-cellular layer surrounding the primary oocyte inside the Graafian follicle.
**Question 20:** Give one example of an autoimmune disease in humans.

## SECTION D (15 Marks)
*Answer any ONE question from each of the three pairs below. Each question carries 5 marks.*

**Question 35:**
**(Option A)**

**(Option B)**

**(Option B)**

**Question 37:**
**(Option A)**
Trace the development of a typical dicot embryo from the zygote stage to the mature embryo stage. Support your answer with diagrams showing the globular, heart-shaped, and mature stages.
**OR**
**(Option B)**
