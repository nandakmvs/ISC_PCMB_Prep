## SECTION A (5 Marks)
*Answer all questions. Each question carries 1 mark.*

**Question 1:** If $A$ is a square matrix of order 3 and $|A| = -2$, find the value of $|-3A|$.
**Question 2:** Find the value of $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{4}{5} + \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{5}$. *(Do not make careless addition errors).*
**Question 3:** What is the principal value branch of $\cos^{-1}x$?
**Question 4:** Find $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ if $\vec{a} = \hat{i}-2\hat{j}+\hat{k}$ and $\vec{b} = 3\hat{i}+\hat{j}-2\hat{k}$.
**Question 5:** Write the formula for $P(B|A)$ in terms of intersections.

---

## SECTION B (10 Marks)
*Answer all questions. Each question carries 2 marks.*

**Question 6:** Prove that the relation $R$ on the set of real numbers defined as $R = \{(a,b) : a \le b^2\}$ is **not** transitive by providing a clear numerical counter-example.
**Question 7:** Differentiate $y = \sin^{-1}(2x\sqrt{1-x^2})$ with respect to x.
**Question 8:** Find a unit vector perpendicular to both $\vec{a} = \hat{i}+\hat{j}$ and $\vec{b} = \hat{j}+\hat{k}$.
**Question 9:** Without expanding, evaluate $\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 5 & 6 & 8 \\ 6x & 9x & 12x \end{vmatrix}$.
**Question 10:** If $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 2 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{bmatrix} a & 1 \\ b & -1 \end{bmatrix}$, find $a$ and $b$ such that $(A+B)^2 = A^2 + B^2$.

## SECTION C (15 Marks)
*Answer all questions. Each question carries 3 marks.*

**Question 11:** Discuss the continuity of the function $f(x)$ at $x = 2$.
$$f(x) = \begin{cases} \frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2}, & \text{if } x \neq 2 \\ 4, & \text{if } x = 2 \end{cases}$$
*(Warning: You MUST explicitly use $x \to 2^-$ and $x \to 2^+$ limit notations).*

**Question 12:** Find the intervals in which the function $f(x) = 2x^3 - 3x^2 - 36x + 7$ is strictly increasing or strictly decreasing. *(Do not leave this blank!)*

**Question 13:** If $y = e^{a \cos^{-1} x}$, prove that $(1-x^2)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} - x\frac{dy}{dx} - a^2y = 0$. *(Do not stop halfway!)*

**Question 14:** Prove that $\tan^{-1} \frac{1}{4} + \tan^{-1} \frac{2}{9} = \frac{1}{2} \cos^{-1} \frac{3}{5}$.

**Question 15:** A man is known to speak the truth 3 out of 4 times. He throws a die and reports that it is a six. Find the probability that it is actually a six.
