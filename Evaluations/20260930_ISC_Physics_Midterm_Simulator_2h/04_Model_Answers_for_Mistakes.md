# Model Answers & Corrections: Physics UT-1 2025

### 1. The Missing Diagram (Gauss's Law for Infinite Wire)
**Mistake:** Executing the derivation without the supporting Gaussian surface diagram.
**Correction:** 
Whenever deriving the electric field due to a line charge, you MUST draw a straight line of charge (labeled $+\lambda$), surrounded by a coaxial cylindrical Gaussian surface of radius $r$ and length $l$.
- Clearly label the electric field vector $\vec{E}$ pointing radially outward.
- Clearly label the area vectors $d\vec{A}$ on the curved surface (parallel to $\vec{E}$) and on the flat circular caps (perpendicular to $\vec{E}$).
*Only then* can you write $\oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{A} = E \times 2\pi rl = \frac{\lambda l}{\epsilon_0}$.

### 2. The Mirror vs. Lens Confusion
**Mistake:** Using the lens formula but referring to the device as a mirror.
**Correction:**
- **Lenses** use refraction. Formula: $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$. Magnification: $m = +\frac{v}{u}$. An image with $+v$ is formed on the *other side* of the lens (real).
- **Mirrors** use reflection. Formula: $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$. Magnification: $m = -\frac{v}{u}$. An image with $+v$ is formed *behind the mirror* (virtual).
Always read the question carefully to identify if the optical device is a lens or a mirror before selecting your formula and phrasing your final position statement.

### 3. Blank Strategy (How to avoid blanks)
**Mistake:** Leaving Q11(b) blank after writing $n = 1.5$.
**Correction (Brain Dump Rule):** If you see $n=1.5$ in an optics question and you are stuck, write down:
- Snell's Law: $n_1 \sin i = n_2 \sin r$
- Lens Maker's Formula: $\frac{1}{f} = (n_{21} - 1)\left(\frac{1}{R_1} - \frac{1}{R_2}\right)$
Writing one of these formulas correctly will earn you at least 0.5 or 1 step mark from an examiner, compared to a guaranteed 0 for a blank.
