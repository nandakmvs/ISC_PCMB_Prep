## SECTION A: Objective Questions (10 Marks)
*Answer all questions. Each question carries 1 mark.*

**1.** Two point charges $+4q$ and $+q$ are placed at a distance $L$ apart. A third charge $Q$ is placed on the line joining them such that the system is in equilibrium. What is the position of $Q$ from $+4q$?
(a) $L/2$
(b) $2L/3$
(c) $L/3$
(d) $L/4$

**2.** The electric potential $V$ at any point $(x, y, z)$ is given by $V = 4x^2$ volts. The electric field at the point $(1, 0, 2)$ is:
(a) $8 \text{ V/m}$ along negative x-axis
(b) $8 \text{ V/m}$ along positive x-axis
(c) $16 \text{ V/m}$ along negative x-axis
(d) $16 \text{ V/m}$ along positive x-axis

**3.** In a Wheatstone bridge, if the battery and galvanometer are interchanged, the null point:
(a) Shifts to the left
(b) Shifts to the right
(c) Remains unchanged
(d) Cannot be determined

**4.** A convex lens of glass ($\mu = 1.5$) has a focal length of $20 \text{ cm}$ in air. If it is immersed in water ($\mu = 1.33$), its focal length will:
(a) Increase
(b) Decrease
(c) Remain the same
(d) Become negative

**5.** In Young's Double Slit Experiment (YDSE), if the distance between the slits is halved and the distance from the slits to the screen is doubled, the fringe width becomes:
(a) Half
(b) Double
(c) Four times
(d) One-fourth

**6.** Which of the following wavefronts is associated with a point source at a finite distance?
(a) Spherical
(b) Cylindrical
(c) Plane
(d) Elliptical

**7.** The core of an optical fiber is made of material with refractive index $\mu_1$ and the cladding with refractive index $\mu_2$. For total internal reflection to occur:
(a) $\mu_1 < \mu_2$
(b) $\mu_1 > \mu_2$
(c) $\mu_1 = \mu_2$
(d) None of the above

**8.** Three capacitors of capacitances $2\mu F, 3\mu F$, and $6\mu F$ are connected in series. Their equivalent capacitance is:
(a) $11 \mu F$
(b) $1 \mu F$
(c) $1.2 \mu F$
(d) $2 \mu F$

**9.** The drift velocity of electrons in a conductor is proportional to:
(a) The electric field $E$
(b) The square of the electric field $E^2$
(c) $\sqrt{E}$
(d) $1/E$

**10.** According to Gauss's Law, the electric flux through a closed surface enclosing a dipole is:
(a) $\frac{q}{\epsilon_0}$
(b) $\frac{2q}{\epsilon_0}$
(c) Zero
(d) Infinity

**16.** Using Gauss's Law, derive an expression for the electric field intensity due to a uniformly charged infinite plane sheet. **(Must include a labeled diagram).**
**17.** Derive the relation between internal resistance ($r$), electromotive force ($E$), and terminal voltage ($V$) of a cell connected to an external resistance ($R$).
