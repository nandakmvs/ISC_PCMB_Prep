## SECTION A: Objective Questions (10 Marks)
*Answer all questions. Each question carries 1 mark.*

**1.** Which of the following aqueous solutions will have the highest boiling point? (Assume complete dissociation).
(a) $0.1 \text{ M } NaCl$
(b) $0.1 \text{ M } BaCl_2$
(c) $0.1 \text{ M } Al_2(SO_4)_3$
(d) $0.1 \text{ M } Urea$

**2.** The primary valency and secondary valency of the central metal ion in $[Co(NH_3)_5Cl]Cl_2$ are respectively:
(a) 3 and 6
(b) 2 and 5
(c) 3 and 5
(d) 2 and 6

**3.** Which of the following alkyl halides will undergo $S_N1$ reaction most readily?
(a) $(CH_3)_3C-F$
(b) $(CH_3)_3C-Cl$
(c) $(CH_3)_3C-Br$
(d) $(CH_3)_3C-I$

**4.** The reagent used in the Clemmensen reduction is:
(a) $Zn-Hg / conc. HCl$
(b) $NH_2NH_2 / KOH$
(c) $LiAlH_4$
(d) $Na / C_2H_5OH$

**5.** Which of the following does NOT reduce Tollens' reagent?
(a) Formic acid
(b) Acetaldehyde
(c) Benzaldehyde
(d) Acetone

**6.** Which base is present in RNA but not in DNA?
(a) Adenine
(b) Guanine
(c) Uracil
(d) Thymine

**7.** The basic character of amines in aqueous solution follows the order (for methyl amines):
(a) $1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ$
(b) $2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ$
(c) $3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ$
(d) $2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ$

**(Questions 8 to 10: Assertion-Reasoning)**
*Choose the correct option from the following:*
*(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.*
*(b) Both A and R are true, but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.*
*(c) Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false.*
*(d) Assertion (A) is false but Reason (R) is true.*

**8. Assertion (A):** Transition metals and their compounds act as good catalysts.
**Reason (R):** Transition metals have variable oxidation states and can provide a large surface area for the adsorption of reactant molecules.

**9. Assertion (A):** Optically active 2-bromobutane on treatment with aqueous $KOH$ undergoes racemization.
**Reason (R):** The reaction proceeds via an $S_N2$ mechanism resulting in inversion of configuration.

**10. Assertion (A):** Amino acids behave like salts rather than simple amines or carboxylic acids.
**Reason (R):** In aqueous solution, the carboxyl group can lose a proton and the amino group can accept a proton to form a Zwitterion.
