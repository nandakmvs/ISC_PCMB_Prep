# Model Answers & Corrections: Chemistry Midterm Simulator 02

### 1. Organic Conversion: Aniline to p-Bromoaniline
**Mistake:** Writing only the reactant and product without reagents. Direct bromination of aniline yields 2,4,6-tribromoaniline because the $-NH_2$ group is highly activating.
**Correction (The 3-Step Protection Method):**
1. **Protection:** React aniline with acetic anhydride ($(CH_3CO)_2O$) in pyridine to form acetanilide. This reduces the activating power of the amino group.
2. **Bromination:** React acetanilide with $Br_2$ in acetic acid to get p-bromoacetanilide (major product due to steric hindrance at ortho).
3. **Hydrolysis:** Hydrolyze with dilute $HCl$ or $NaOH$ to remove the acetyl group, yielding p-bromoaniline.

### 2. Mechanism: Acid-Catalyzed Dehydration of Ethanol to Ethene
**Mistake:** Skipping the carbocation intermediate.
**Correction:**
This mechanism has 3 distinct steps that must be written explicitly:
- **Step 1: Protonation of alcohol.** 
  $CH_3CH_2OH + H^+ \rightleftharpoons CH_3CH_2O^+H_2$ (Fast)
- **Step 2: Formation of carbocation.** 
  The protonated alcohol loses a water molecule. This is the **slow, rate-determining step**.
  $CH_3CH_2O^+H_2 \rightarrow CH_3CH_2^+ + H_2O$
- **Step 3: Formation of ethene.** 
  The carbocation loses a proton to form a double bond.
  $CH_3CH_2^+ \rightarrow CH_2=CH_2 + H^+$ (Fast)
