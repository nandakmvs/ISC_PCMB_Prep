**(Questions 8 to 10: Assertion-Reasoning)**
*Choose the correct option from the following:*
*(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.*
*(b) Both A and R are true, but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.*
*(c) Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false.*
*(d) Assertion (A) is false but Reason (R) is true.*

**8. Assertion (A):** The boiling point of a $0.1 \text{ M}$ solution of $NaCl$ is higher than that of a $0.1 \text{ M}$ solution of glucose.
**Reason (R):** Elevation in boiling point is a colligative property and depends on the number of particles in solution.

**9. Assertion (A):** $[Co(NH_3)_6]^{3+}$ is an inner orbital complex and is diamagnetic.
**Reason (R):** $NH_3$ is a strong field ligand and causes pairing of electrons in the 3d orbitals of $Co^{3+}$.

**10. Assertion (A):** Aniline does not undergo Friedel-Crafts reaction.
**Reason (R):** The $-NH_2$ group of aniline reacts with the Lewis acid catalyst $AlCl_3$ to form a salt, deactivating the ring.
